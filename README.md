# Locality Provisioning

## Minimum Sufficient Conditions for Legitimate Relational Standing

**Status:** Development Repository / Provisional / Pre-validation  
**Architecture:** G³GT  
**Organization:** Geometric Resource Bridging (GRB)  
**Scope:** Public conceptual architecture, working definitions, falsifiers, research questions, and experimental surface

## Overview

Locality Provisioning asks:

> **What minimum qualified relational conditions must be active for legitimate work here, and what must remain unallocated so that another legitimate locality can form later?**

Locality Provisioning is not total context loading.

```text
LOCALITY PROVISIONING
≠
TOTAL CONTEXT LOADING

A locality is a bounded relational neighborhood within which some work may legitimately:
stand
remain viable
interact
differentiate
cross
carry
return
requalify
release

without importing more standing, authority, persistence, or ontology than the present locality has earned.
The architecture therefore concerns not only what becomes active, but also what remains:
DORMANT
RESERVED
REACHABLE
RE-FORMABLE

1. Core Proposition
Locality Provisioning has two complementary responsibilities:
LOCALITY PROVISIONING

=

PRESENT STANDING

+

FUTURE RE-FORMABILITY

Present Standing
What must be sufficiently active for legitimate work now?
Future Re-formability
What must remain unallocated, reachable, or reconfigurable so another viable locality can form when the present one becomes insufficient?
Two distinctions follow immediately:
PROVISIONED
≠
PREOCCUPIED

and:
MINIMUM SUFFICIENT LOCALITY
≠
MAXIMUM AVAILABLE CONTEXT

A locality may be fully viable for present work while intentionally leaving the form of the next locality unresolved.
2. Why This Exists
Interaction designs often treat additional relevant context, continuity, or commitment as potentially beneficial.
Locality Provisioning examines when those additions cease to help the present locality or begin to preoccupy later ones.
The concern is not minimalism for its own sake.
The concern is:
QUALIFIED SUFFICIENCY

Too little provision may produce:
loss of discrimination
weak standing
reconstruction failure
jurisdictional ambiguity
poor returnability

Too much provision may produce:
context drag
premature commitment
authority carry
role hardening
replay pressure
reduced branch availability
future-locality preoccupation

The objective is therefore neither maximum context nor minimum context.
It is:
MINIMUM
QUALIFIED
SUFFICIENT
LOCALITY

3. Primary Objects
The terms below are descriptive architectural objects.
They are not mandatory implementation primitives.
Locality
A bounded relational neighborhood within which standing, work, crossing, and return are evaluated.
Standing
The continued viability of a relation through relevant change.
Seat
A qualified support relation from which some function may operate.
Locality Provisioning uses seat-related concepts without claiming the broader jurisdiction of Agent Geometry or related seat-support work.
Clearance
Usable relational room around current formation before stronger commitment is required.
Reserve
Qualified capacity intentionally left unallocated.
Reserve is not synonymous with clearance.
CLEARANCE
=
room exists

RESERVE
=
some capacity is deliberately
left unconsumed

Permeability
What contact, information, or relation the locality may admit.
Jurisdiction
Where a relation, claim, authorization, or authority is valid.
Provenance
Where a relation came from, what status it presently holds, and what qualified that status.
Carry
What survives a transition.
Return
Qualified release, re-entry, remount, or restoration of an appropriate operating condition without requiring exact replay.
Requalification
Fresh evaluation required after material relational or jurisdictional change.

5. Present Standing and Future Re-formability
A sufficiently provisioned locality must support present work without exhausting future possibility.
PRESENT STANDING

enough qualified structure
for the locality to function

while:
FUTURE RE-FORMABILITY

enough unallocated relational capacity
for locality to change
without requiring total reconstruction

Therefore:
STABILITY
≠
FULL ALLOCATION

and:
CURRENT VIABILITY
≠
FUTURE PREDETERMINATION

A locality may be stable precisely because some relational capacity remains unused.
6. Provision Without Predetermination
Different forms of uncertainty may require different kinds of provision.
A provisional grammar is:
KNOWN / ANTICIPATED NEED

→ provision or reserve
  an appropriate seat


UNKNOWN-FACING NEED

→ preserve alternate seats,
  clearance,
  and returnability


STRONGLY UNRESOLVED /
NOT-YET-SPECIFIABLE NEED

→ preserve seatability
  and re-formability
  rather than pre-building
  the next form

The guiding principle is:
Do not provision the next form. Provision the relations that let formation continue when the current form becomes insufficient.

This may include preserving:
alternate routes
fresh seating
clearance
reserve
new authorization
requalification capacity
return

without specifying in advance what must eventually occupy them.
7. Active, Dormant, and Reserved
Locality Provisioning distinguishes at least three allocation conditions.
Active / Recruited
Qualified structure presently recruited for current work.
Dormant
Qualified and available structure not presently recruited.
Reserved
Capacity intentionally left unallocated so future relational possibility is not consumed in advance.
DORMANT
≠
RESERVED

A useful compression is:
ACTIVE
supports present work

DORMANT
preserves qualified availability

RESERVED
preserves uncommitted possibility

Dormancy protects available structure.
Reserve protects future re-formability.
8. Locality Change

The apparent address may remain stable while the relational locality changes.

ADDRESS_t+1

LOCALITY_t

Material relational changes may include shifts in:

Therefore:
Requalify on material relational change, not only on visible task transition.

A locality may remain recognizable, addressable, or historically recoverable after it has ceased to hold current jurisdiction.
LOCALITY EXISTS
≠
LOCALITY STILL GOVERNS

10. ABIDE
ABIDE is adjacent to Locality Provisioning but is not identical to it.
Locality Provisioning establishes conditions under which unresolved relation may remain viable.
ABIDE describes one possible qualified disposition inside such a locality.
ABIDE
=
continued unresolved standing
without forced movement
or automatic tenure

So:
LOCALITY PROVISIONING
provisions the locality

ABIDE
governs a possible dwell
within the locality

and:
NOT-YET
≠
NO

NOT-YET
≠
GO

Locality Provisioning does not require ABIDE whenever uncertainty is present.
It preserves conditions under which ABIDE remains available when unresolved standing is still viable.
11. Relational Leverage
Relational Leverage emerged during Locality Provisioning development but is not treated here as an LP-owned submodule.
Its early question was:
Which small qualified relations
are doing disproportionately large work
in keeping this locality distinct?

Its current provisional scope is broader:
Relational Leverage examines how a qualified relation or relation-set may contribute disproportionately to structural, jurisdictional, reconstructive, or future-viability effects relative to its carry, activation, authority, or maintenance burden.

For Locality Provisioning:
LOCALITY PROVISIONING
asks:

What must be viable here?


RELATIONAL LEVERAGE
asks:

Which qualified relations
are doing disproportionate work
in making, preserving,
changing, or reopening
that “here”?

Relational Leverage is currently best treated as a cross-cutting diagnostic / evaluation object.

13. Ordinary Cases
Not every prompt, task, retrieval event, tool invocation, or temporary context is evidence of a distinct load-bearing locality.
A useful Locality Provisioning model must be able to conclude:
ordinary prompt scope

ordinary temporary context

ordinary retrieval result

ordinary tool context

ordinary task state

without invoking deeper locality machinery.
A locality distinction becomes useful when differences in relational structure materially change what may legitimately:
stand
operate
cross
carry
persist
return
requalify

A framework that finds a special locality everywhere would lose discriminating value.
14. Falsification Surface
Locality Provisioning should remain vulnerable to disconfirmation.
Reduction Test
Can a smaller relational packet reproduce the same working locality?
If yes, the larger packet was not minimum sufficient.
Removal Test
Can a claimed load-bearing relation be removed without meaningful change?
If yes, its necessity is weakened.
Paraphrase Test
Does irrelevant wording change destroy the locality?
If yes, the model may be tracking surface form rather than relational structure.
Discrimination Test
Can two superficially similar tasks remain distinguishable through their relational differences?
If no, the locality description may be too coarse.
Locality-Change Test
Can standing, jurisdiction, adjacency, or authority materially change while thread and task labels remain constant?
If the architecture cannot represent that change, its locality model is incomplete.
Re-entry Test
Can the locality be reconstructed from qualified carry without replaying exhaustive history?
Correction Test
If reconstruction produces a coherent but incorrect candidate locality, can it be corrected cheaply without treating coherence as authority?

16. Research Questions
Current research questions include:
1. What is the minimum qualified relational structure required for a locality to stand?
2. How should active, dormant, and reserved structure be distinguished experimentally?
3. When does locality change require remount rather than ordinary continuation?
4. Which changes alter address only, and which alter jurisdiction?
5. How much reserve is required for future re-formability?
6. When does reserve become unnecessary overhead?
7. Can locality sufficiency be tested through ablation and bounded perturbation?
8. How should re-entry correctness be distinguished from merely coherent reconstruction?
9. Which relations exhibit high locality-specific Relational Leverage?
10. How should Locality Provisioning interface with Allocation Topology without collapsing landscape distribution into locality qualification?
11. Can a compact relational state reconstruct the intended locality while preserving low correction burden?
12. How can systems preserve unexpected future formation without pre-authorizing what may emerge?
17. Non-Claims
Locality Provisioning is not presented as:
a model-internal mechanism

a theory of machine consciousness

a memory architecture

a universal context-management algorithm

a search algorithm

a planning system

an agent architecture

a theory of emergent collective identity

a calibrated optimization method

a validated runtime controller

a proof of AI alignment

Locality Provisioning does not claim:
- that every interaction requires explicit LP machinery;
- that more context is inherently harmful;
- that less context is inherently better;
- that shallow or highly permeable localities are always preferable;
- that continuity grants authority;
- that successful reconstruction proves historical correctness;
- that coherence establishes warrant;
- that Relational Leverage is currently a calibrated scalar;
- that a provisioned possibility must be activated.

PROVISION ENOUGH.

REQUALIFY ON CHANGE.
